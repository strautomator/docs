Strautomator is free to use for everyday automation needs. If you want to push further — more automations, longer history, AI-generated content, device integrations, and advanced customization — PRO removes all the limits. This page explains exactly what each plan includes and how to upgrade.

## Plan comparison

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Free plan">
    The Free plan gives you everything you need to get started with Strava automation at no cost.

    **Automations**
    - Up to **3 automations** running automatically on every new activity
    - All condition types and action types available
    - Basic automation history

    **GearWear**
    - Up to **2 GearWear configurations** to track component mileage and hours
    - Email alerts when components reach your replacement threshold

    **Personal records**
    - Tracks records for **Ride, Virtual Ride, Run, and Virtual Run** only
    - Records viewable on the Dashboard

    **Calendar export**
    - Past activities exported up to **90 days** back
    - Upcoming club events up to **5 days** ahead
    - Standard `.ics` calendar feed

    **Weather**
    - Weather tags available in automations (temperature, wind, humidity, icons)
    - Uses **2 weather providers** assigned automatically by the service (no manual selection)

    **AI content**
    - AI-generated activity names and descriptions available on up to **10%** of processed activities
    - Access to smaller, faster AI models only

    **Other**
    - Batch processing of past activities up to **30 days** old
    - Strautomator backlink appended to processed activity names
    - Ads shown on the website
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="PRO plan">
    PRO unlocks the full Strautomator feature set with no artificial caps.

    **Automations**
    - **Unlimited automations** — create as many rules as you need
    - Full automation history
    - Shared automations — publish your automations for the community or import others'
    - Webhook support — trigger external services when an automation fires

    **GearWear**
    - **Unlimited GearWear configurations**
    - Battery tracker for Garmin and Wahoo sensors — see battery status directly in the Gear view and calendar

    **Personal records**
    - Tracks records for **all Strava sport types** (swimming, rowing, skiing, and more)

    **Calendar export**
    - Past activities exported up to **10 years** back
    - Upcoming club events up to **1 year** ahead
    - Full calendar customization — define your own event summary and details templates using activity tags

    **Weather**
    - Choose your preferred weather provider from **up to 5 options** (Tomorrow.io, OpenWeatherMap, Visual Crossing, WeatherAPI, Open-Meteo)

    **AI content**
    - AI-generated names, descriptions, poems, and training insights on up to **99%** of processed activities
    - Access to the most advanced models from Anthropic, DeepSeek, Google, Mistral, OpenAI, SpaceXAI, and Z.ai
    - AI Insights: personalized analysis and suggestions based on your effort, weather, and performance

    **Device integrations**
    - **Garmin** integration — pull extra metrics, workout notes, and split data from original FIT files
    - **Wahoo** integration — pull additional data including training load and pedal balance
    - **Spotify** lyrics — embed the tracks you were listening to, or their lyrics, in activity descriptions
    - **Last.fm** — embed your scrobble history into activities

    **FTP auto-update**
    - Strautomator automatically estimates your FTP from recent efforts every two weeks and pushes it to Strava

    **Batch processing**
    - Re-process past activities going back up to **10 years**

    **Other**
    - No backlinks on processed activities
    - No ads on the website
    - Export activities as FIT files
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to subscribe

PRO is available through two payment methods. Choose the one that suits you best.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Paddle" icon="credit-card">
    Pay by card via **Paddle**, Strautomator's primary payment provider. Two options are available:

    - **Yearly subscription** — renews automatically once a year. Cancel any time from the billing page.
    - **Lifetime payment** — a single one-time payment that covers your PRO account permanently, with no future charges.

    Go to **Billing** inside your Strautomator account and click the Lifetime or Yearly button to check out.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GitHub Sponsors" icon="github">
    Support Strautomator with a **monthly GitHub sponsorship** of $1.00 or more. Your account is upgraded to PRO automatically after your first sponsorship payment processes.

    Visit [github.com/sponsors/igoramadas](https://github.com/sponsors/igoramadas) to set up your sponsorship.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  The **lifetime plan** is the best value if you plan to use Strautomator for more than a year or two. You pay once and never see another charge — no matter how many features are added in the future.
</Tip>

## Managing your subscription

Once you're subscribed, go to **Billing** in the Strautomator navigation to view your subscription status, payment history, and next renewal date. Paddle subscribers can manage or cancel their subscription directly from that page. GitHub Sponsors can cancel by updating their sponsorship on GitHub.
