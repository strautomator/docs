Shared automations let you publish any of your automations as a template that other Strautomator users can copy into their own accounts with a single click. If you find an automation that does something useful, you can adopt it without having to recreate the conditions and actions from scratch.

## Who can do what

<Note>
  **Creating and sharing** an automation requires a PRO subscription. **Using** someone else's shared automation is available to all users, including free accounts.
</Note>

This means you can discover and copy community automations even on the free plan — a great way to get started with powerful setups before deciding whether to upgrade.

---

## How to share one of your automations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the automation editor">
    Go to **Automations** and click the automation you want to share to open its editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Share button">
    Find the **Share** button at the top or bottom of the editor. Strautomator creates a unique public URL for that automation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the share URL">
    Copy the URL and send it to whoever you want — paste it in a forum post, a club chat, social media, or anywhere else.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once shared, the automation is publicly accessible via its URL. Anyone who visits the link can preview it and copy it to their account.

---

## What gets shared (and what doesn't)

Sharing is designed to be safe. When you share an automation, Strautomator **strips all personal data** before publishing it:

- GPS coordinates in location conditions are removed or anonymized.
- Gear IDs (specific bike or shoe IDs from your Strava account) are not included.
- Your personal account details are not attached to the shared automation.

The shared automation contains only the structure — condition types and logic, action types — so others can customize the values for their own setup after copying.

---

## How to use someone else's shared automation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the share URL">
    Open the link shared by another athlete. You'll see a preview of the automation — its name, conditions, and actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Use this automation'">
    If you want to add it to your account, click the **Use this automation** button. Strautomator copies the automation into your account as a new, independent automation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize it for your setup">
    Open the newly copied automation in your editor. Update any conditions or actions that reference personal values — for example, set your own gear, adjust location pins, or change target distances to match your training.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable and save">
    Enable the automation and save. It will run on your next Strava activity that matches the conditions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Copying a shared automation creates a fully independent copy. Changes you make to your copy do not affect the original, and changes the original owner makes later do not update your copy.

## Managing your shared automations

To see all the automations you have shared:

1. Go to **Automations** in the main navigation.
2. Click the **Shared** tab.

From here you can see each shared automation, update it (to reflect changes you've made locally), or delete the share link to make it no longer accessible.

<Info>
  Updating a shared automation from the Shared tab publishes the latest version to the same URL. Users who already copied the old version are not affected — their copies remain unchanged.
</Info>

## Community templates

Strautomator also maintains a set of official community templates visible from the **New Automation** screen. These are curated shared automations that cover popular use cases — renaming commutes, assigning gear by sport type, adding weather to descriptions, and more. Browse them as a source of inspiration or copy them directly as a starting point.
